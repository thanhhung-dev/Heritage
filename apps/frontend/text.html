Weaverville Joss House State Historic Park

A rare 19th-century Taoist temple highlighting Chinese American history and resilience
Weaverville Joss House State Historic Park preserves one of North America's oldest Taoist temples. Rooted in Chinese immigrant history, it remains an active place of religious practice and a vital testament to community resilience, and cultural continuity in northern California.

          About

          This virtual experience is a collaboration between CyArk, California State Parks, and Parks California. Utilizing 3D documentation of the Weaverville Joss House, this virtual experience provides an educational resource where virtual visitors can learn about Chinese American history and Taoist philosophies through exploration of the temple that has been utilized for over 150 years.
