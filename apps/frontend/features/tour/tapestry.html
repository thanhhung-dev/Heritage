About

In this virtual tour of the Ad Deir Monastery, explore one of Petra’s most iconic monuments through the voices of those who know it best. Blending expert insight, local knowledge, and immersive soundscapes, the experience reveals how the Nabataeans shaped this desert site through trade, water engineering, and cultural exchange. Discover the living history of Petra, its past, present, and the communities working to protect its future.
